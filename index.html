      County (point's actual location)

      Unlike the Physiographic Region filter above, this checks each point's real coordinates against Nevada county boundaries — not a taxon-level tag. 14,804 of 14,816 points resolved with confidence; a handful of records with clearly bad source coordinates (e.g. off the coast, or in another state) are excluded rather than guessed at.

      iNat GPS Accuracy

      Obscured points are randomized by iNat (often 10–20+ km) to protect sensitive species. The accuracy filter above now correctly reflects this — obscured points report their true (large) public uncertainty, not the device's original precision — but checking this box adds an explicit, unambiguous exclusion. Affects iNat points only; herbarium records are unaffected by either control.
